About Next Millionaire

A Co-operative Built on Shared Values

Next Millionaire Co-operative was founded with a simple belief: when people work together with shared ownership and mutual accountability, extraordinary things happen. We unite members, consumers, and entrepreneurs into a resilient economic community.

Our democratic structure ensures every member has a voice. Profits are reinvested into the community, creating lasting impact across generations.

Rent a Car. Drive Your Way to Income.

We've built our own fleet step by step — now 10 cars strong and growing every month. Each vehicle is available for commercial rent on a simple monthly lease: you drive it for income, and you pay us monthly. No large upfront purchase, no long-term commitment.
